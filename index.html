PROCESS

From idea to a running system

A fixed sequence for every build, so you always know what stage a project is at.

01

Define the rules

We turn your idea — or an existing strategy — into a precise, unambiguous rule set: entries, exits, position sizing, filters.

02

Build the EA or indicator

Coded natively in MQL4, MQL5 or Pine Script, matching the platform your strategy needs to run on.

03

Backtest & optimise

Run against historical data across market conditions, with results reviewed together before anything goes live.

04

Deploy & hand over

Delivered with the logic documented, ready to run on your account or chart, with support for any adjustments.
